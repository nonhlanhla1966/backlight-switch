        How it works

        1. Grant both permissions above (one-time, system screens).
        2. Set a comfortable global brightness on the Brightness tab.
        3. In Per-App, tap an app and choose its own brightness.
        4. Add a Schedule to dim automatically at set times, and Sensor rules to dim while an app makes the phone hot.
        5. Turn the top switch on. Opening a ruled app applies its level; closing it restores your previous level automatically.

        A quiet notification keeps the watcher alive; it pauses whenever the screen is off. Auto-brightness starts again after reboot if it was enabled.
